Program pillar 9

Advancing Equity, Inclusion & Human Rights

Every person in every community we serve deserves dignity, opportunity, and protection — regardless of gender, background, or circumstance.

Our approach

This pillar underpins all of our work. Across our health, education, environmental, and livelihoods programs, we actively work to ensure equitable access and outcomes — particularly for women, girls, refugees, returnees, and other groups who face structural barriers.

Focus areas

  • Gender equality integrated across all ten program pillars
  • Economic empowerment programs specifically designed for single mothers
  • Inclusion and support for refugees, returnees, and displaced communities
  • Advocacy for the rights of marginalized groups within the communities we serve

Why it matters

65% of our beneficiaries are women — a reflection of both the need and our intentional focus. Sustainable development cannot happen while entire groups remain excluded from opportunity. This pillar ensures equity isn't an afterthought, but a thread woven through everything we do.
