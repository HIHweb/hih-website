Art, Culture & Heritage

Development that forgets culture forgets identity. We celebrate and preserve Burundian heritage as part of building resilient communities.

Our approach

Alongside our health, education, and economic programs, Holistic Impact Hub invests in preserving and celebrating Burundian culture — traditional music, storytelling, craft, and communal practices that strengthen identity and social cohesion.

Focus areas

  • Community storytelling and oral history preservation
  • Traditional arts and craft training, including for economic empowerment
  • Cultural events that bring communities together, including reconciliation programs
  • Youth engagement with cultural heritage as a source of pride and identity

Why it matters

Culture is a foundation for resilience. By honoring Burundian heritage alongside our development work, we help communities build futures that are rooted in — not disconnected from — their own identity and history.
