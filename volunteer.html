Volunteer With Us

Share your time and skills with communities across Burundi.

Holistic Impact Hub is a community-driven non-profit based in Bujumbura. Since 2020, we've worked alongside refugees, returnees, and vulnerable communities. Volunteers are a big part of that work.

Ways to volunteer

  • Community programs: support water, sanitation, and hygiene (WASH) activities and health outreach.
  • Youth and education: mentor young people and help with leadership and skills training.
  • Peacebuilding: help with community dialogue and social cohesion activities.
  • Communications: photography, writing, translation (Kirundi, French, English), and social media.
  • Professional skills: IT, finance, project management, fundraising, and research.

Who can apply

  • Anyone 18 or older who shares our values of dignity, respect, and community-led change
  • Local and international volunteers, in person or remote
  • No experience needed for community roles

How to apply

  1. Email info@holisticimpacthub.org with the subject "Volunteer Application".
  2. Include your name, country, skills, and availability.
  3. Tell us in a few lines why you want to volunteer.

We'll reply to discuss where you can help most.
