Ten integrated pillars

Practical programs, rooted in real needs

Every program is designed with the communities it serves, addressing interconnected challenges rather than isolated symptoms.

1. Health & Wellbeing

Expanding access to vital healthcare services, mental health support, maternal health education, and community wellbeing initiatives in underserved areas.

2. Education & Empowerment

Essential literacy and numeracy support, STEM education, and creative programs that foster gender equality and inclusion in schools and communities.

3. Environmental Stewardship

Climate-smart agricultural practices, reforestation, watershed protection, and environmental resilience training for vulnerable communities.

4. Sustainable Livelihoods

Local entrepreneurship training, microenterprise support, and income-generation programs — with a focus on economic empowerment for single mothers.

5. Water, Sanitation & Hygiene (WASH)

Clean water access, sanitation infrastructure, and hygiene education to reduce preventable illness in communities across Burundi.

6. Child Nutrition Support

Nutrition screening, supplementary feeding programs, and caregiver education to address and prevent childhood malnutrition.

7. Peacebuilding & Social Cohesion

Community dialogue, reconciliation initiatives, and inclusion programs supporting refugees, returnees, and host communities alike.

8. Youth Empowerment

Mentorship, leadership training, and skills development that equip young people — 600+ trained so far — to shape their own futures.

9. Advancing Equity, Inclusion & Human Rights

Programs and advocacy that protect and advance the rights of women, girls, and marginalized groups across our communities. Learn more →

10. Art, Culture & Heritage

Preserving and celebrating Burundian culture through community art, storytelling, and heritage initiatives. Learn more →
