Who we are

Building resilient communities, together

Founded in Bujumbura in 2020, Holistic Impact Hub exists to walk alongside communities across Burundi and the wider Great Lakes region — not as an outside solution, but as a committed partner in lasting change.

Our story

Holistic Impact Hub (HIH) was founded in 2020 in response to the interconnected challenges facing communities in Burundi — challenges that no single-issue program could fully address. Rather than focusing on one need in isolation, our founders believed that lasting change required a holistic approach: tackling health, education, environment, and economic opportunity together, shaped by the people who live these realities every day.

Since then, we've grown into a community-driven organization serving 3,500+ people across 12 communities, working alongside 18+ local and international partners, and training more than 600 young people — with 65% of our beneficiaries being women and girls.

Our mission

To foster resilient, thriving, and self-reliant communities in Burundi by integrating health, education, environmental stewardship, and economic empowerment into programs that create lasting, sustainable development — designed with, not for, the people they serve.

Our vision

A Great Lakes region where every community has the tools, opportunities, and dignity to shape its own future — where no one is left behind.

What guides us

  • Community ownership — programs are co-designed with the people they serve, not imposed on them.
  • Integrated impact — real change happens when health, education, environment, and livelihoods are addressed together.
  • Transparency — donors, partners, and communities alike deserve to see exactly how resources are used.
  • Local capacity — sustainable change means building skills and structures that outlast our direct involvement.

Where we work

HIH operates from our hub in Bujumbura, with active programs across 12 communities in Burundi, and growing partnerships extending into the wider Great Lakes region including the Democratic Republic of Congo and Rwanda.
