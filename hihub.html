HiHub

A verified platform connecting NGOs, donors and communities.

HiHub is built by Holistic Impact Hub to bring transparency and trust to the humanitarian sector across the Great Lakes region (Burundi, DRC, Rwanda and Tanzania). Local organizations build credibility, donors give with confidence, and communities see where support goes.

Open HiHub Ask us a question

For NGOs and local partners

  • Register your organization and submit documents for verification
  • Build a trusted, verified profile that donors can see
  • Share project updates and results with your supporters

For donors and partners

  • Find verified organizations and projects
  • Confirm that support was received with photo, video or location proof
  • Follow the impact of your contribution

For communities

  • A clear, safe way to be heard and to see what support arrives
  • Available in English, French and Swahili
  • Works on your phone and can be installed like an app
